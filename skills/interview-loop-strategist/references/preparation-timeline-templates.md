# Candidate-Controlled Preparation Timeline Templates

These templates are starting points, not prescriptions. Choose the target role, round coverage, hours, recovery time, and any accessibility accommodations with the candidate. Replace unknown interview details with an explicit review point rather than assuming an employer, seniority band, or fixed workload.

## Planning rules

- Use only the hours the candidate chooses and reduce the plan when their constraints change.
- Cover the round types that are known; label every other round as an assumption.
- Pair each practice block with a short debrief and a rest or transition block when useful.
- Change the next cycle only from a candidate request or observable practice evidence.
- Use `ml-system-design-interview` only for an ML-specific target. For general SWE design, use a candidate-selected prompt with `interview-simulator`; no general-design specialist is currently installed.

## One-week reset

Use when an interview is near or details are incomplete.

| Day | Candidate-selected focus | Suggested artifact | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirm target and constraints | Round map with facts, assumptions, and unknowns | Which assumption needs verification first? |
| 2 | Coding or technical reasoning | Timed solution or outline | Which step had the thinnest evidence? |
| 3 | General SWE design or ML design if relevant | Constraint-first design outline | Did the prompt match the target role? |
| 4 | Story evidence | Candidate-authored project facts | Which claim needs verification or removal? |
| 5 | Candidate-selected mock | Consented session record and rubric notes | Which one experiment is worth repeating? |
| 6–7 | Recovery, light review, or rest | Candidate choice | Is a schedule adjustment needed? |

## Four-week cadence

| Week | Goal | Candidate choices | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Establish coverage | Select likely round types and time budget | Assumption-labeled prep plan |
| 2 | Build evidence | Choose one technical and one story practice focus | Story evidence map and one debrief |
| 3 | Rehearse | Choose one or two mocks with desired modality | Evidence-linked experiments |
| 4 | Consolidate | Rehearse only the selected priorities and preserve recovery | Candidate-approved final adjustments |

## Replanning prompts

Use these when the plan changes:

1. What changed in the target, schedule, or available energy?
2. Which completed artifact provides relevant evidence, and what remains unknown?
3. What should be reduced or removed before adding new work?
4. Does the candidate want a different cadence, modality, or feedback style?

Do not use a mock score, inferred confidence, employer preference, or a generic company template to allocate practice time.
