## Known gaps and refresh policy

The complete current handbook was not accessible. The archived PDF and posts with MLA 8 warnings
remain explicitly limited evidence, even where the baseline is familiar. Alphabetization nuances,
run-in/block punctuation edge cases, multi-paragraph extracts, ellipses, drama, archival/legal sources,
complex contributor credits, publisher exclusions, and repeated-author substitutions may require the
current handbook or supplied institutional guide. Do not infer that a general-purpose validator
proves compliance with them. Revisit official edition and AI pages for a new task after substantial
time or whenever the user asks for current guidance. Preserve the date and URL of any new evidence.
