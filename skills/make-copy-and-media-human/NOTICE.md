## Lessons from the Charper example

Charper's October 2026 adaptation included project-specific rules for copy
and imagery. The case illustrates how to check a product's own requirements
during an audience review.

- Verify claims about availability and behavior against product evidence.
- Label mockups, fictional people and model-generated replies where readers
  encounter them. Use language that makes clear what each example represents.
- Check account-deletion claims across surfaces against one documented contract.
- Account for shared screens and inboxes in security notifications.
- Follow the project's size, contrast and motion rules. Verify current product
  wording and policy directly before reusing any historical example.
