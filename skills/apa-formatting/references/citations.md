# APA in-text attribution

Checked 2026-10-25. The examples below are invented illustrations, not evidence.

## Authors and dates

Use author and year: `(Vega, 2024)` or `Vega (2024)`. For two authors use
`(Vega & Noor, 2024)` versus `Vega and Noor (2024)`. With three or more use the
first surname plus `et al.` from the first citation: `(Vega et al., 2024)`.
If different works shorten identically, retain enough surnames to distinguish them.
Spell a group author in full initially; introduce a useful abbreviation once:
`(Coastal Learning Institute [CLI], 2024)`, then `(CLI, 2024)`. Do not invent an
abbreviation for a single mention. Year only belongs in text even if the reference
has a full date. [Author–date rules](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/basic-principles/author-date).

Identical author lists and years need `2024a`, `2024b` consistently in text and
references. Assign letters from reference-list order by title, rather than first
citation order; undated versions use `n.d.-a` and `n.d.-b`.
[Same author/date](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/basic-principles/same-year-author).

When grouping different works in one parenthesis, sort alphabetically and separate
with semicolons: `(Noor, 2023; Vega, 2024)`. For identical authors, give the name
once and order dates from undated, through chronological years, to in press:
`(Noor, n.d., 2022, 2024, in press)`.
[Multiple works](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/basic-principles/multiple-works).

When no author exists, use the title in the author's place as the missing-information
table specifies. For a shortened title's exact in-text italics/quotation marks,
consult the current official no-author guidance or manual Section 8.14 before
finalizing; the dedicated page could not be read during this research. Do not
apply reference-list sentence case blindly to an in-text title.

## Paraphrases and quotations

Paraphrases require attribution even without copied words. Author/year are required;
a page or paragraph locator is optional but helpful for a long source. Make the
scope of a continuing paraphrase clear; cite again when ambiguity arises.
[Paraphrases](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/paraphrasing).

- **Fewer than 40 quoted words:** integrate in text with quotation marks;
  include author/year and a locator. Sentence-final punctuation usually follows
  the closing parenthesis.
- **40 or more:** begin a new double-spaced block, indent the whole passage 0.5
  inch, omit enclosing quotation marks, and put a trailing citation after the
  final punctuation without another period. Do not add extra paragraph spacing.
  Subsequent quoted paragraphs receive an additional first-line indent.

[Quotations](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/quotations).

Never invent a page for an unpaginated source. Use a paragraph number, heading,
or heading plus paragraph; use the quotation's starting timestamp for audiovisual
material. Count actual paragraphs when necessary, not browser display lines.
[No page numbers](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/quotations/no-page-numbers).

## Material you did not retrieve directly

Seek and read the original when possible. If only a secondary source was read,
identify the original in text: `(Vega, 1998, as cited in Noor, 2024)`. Omit the
original year if unknown. Only Noor's actually consulted work enters the reference
list. Do not imply the original was read.
[Secondary sources](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/secondary-sources).

A nonrecoverable personal exchange is cited only in text:
`(A. Noor, personal communication, March 2, 2024)`. Recoverable published exchanges
use their actual source type. Research-participant interviews are research data,
not personal communications; protect confidentiality when reporting them.
[APA Publishing explanation](https://blog.apapubs.org/2018/04/18/apa-style-central-expert-tip-citing-personal-communication/);
[Current missing-information table](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/missing-information).

## Reconciliation pass

Check whether each citation identifies exactly one reference. Compare full author
lists before assigning suffixes; the same first author alone is insufficient.
Check quoted wording and its locator against the supplied original. Record a
source as “metadata verified” separately from “full text read” or “claim verified.”
