## How do citations and Works Cited work?

### Takeaway

MLA keys citations to the entry's first identifying information and a real passage locator; source/container
analysis is more reliable than rigid medium recipes. The bundled source register maps each rule to evidence.

### Cited Findings

- In-text citations use a concise author/title key and supplied passage locator, avoiding duplicated names. — [MLA 9 overview](https://style.mla.org/in-text-citations-overview/)
- Two-author keys require both names; three-plus-author entries/keys use the lead name plus et al. — [Official complex-citation quiz](https://style.mla.org/quiz-complex-in-text-citations/)
- Unknown authors lead to title-first entries; corporate responsibility must be assessed. — [No-author guidance](https://style.mla.org/source-with-no-author/)
- Missing pagination does not justify fabricated numbers; an already identified unpaginated source needs no redundant parenthetical. — [Unpaginated guidance, MLA 8 warning](https://style.mla.org/citing-unpaginated-works/)
- Indirect citations identify the work actually consulted, using qtd. in where appropriate; prefer tracking the original. — [Indirect example, MLA 8 warning](https://style.mla.org/citing-a-quotation-website/)
- The current template orders author, source title, container, contributors, version, number, publisher, date, location and allows additional containers. — [Interactive template](https://style.mla.org/interactive-practice-template/)
- Containment means a work within a larger work, not every URL or browsing tool. Journal/database and stand-alone book examples are supplied by MLA. — [Quick guide](https://style.mla.org/works-cited/works-cited-a-quick-guide/); [practice-template quiz](https://style.mla.org/using-the-practice-template-video-course-quiz/)
- Online location preference is DOI, permalink, URL; use the DOI resolver prefix. — [MLA 9 database guidance](https://style.mla.org/library-database-no-doi/)
- Access dates belong at entry end and help with undated/changed/removed works; do not use n.d. or n. pag. placeholders by default. — [Supplemental elements quiz](https://style.mla.org/mla-style-101-course-quiz-supplemental/); [missing-data guidance](https://style.mla.org/placeholders/)
- Interviewees ordinarily occupy the author role; private recorded interviews can use a note or interview entry, while public recordings use the online version. — [Interviews](https://style.mla.org/interview-in-mla-style/); [recorded interviews](https://style.mla.org/citing-recorded-interview/)
- Clear video creators can be authors; uncertain creators need not be inferred from uploader accounts. — [YouTube guidance](https://style.mla.org/citing-youtube-videos/)

### Inferences

- Model metadata separately from presentation; apply templates only after identifying the actual work and consulted version. This implements MLA's common-elements/container approach. — [Quick guide](https://style.mla.org/works-cited/works-cited-a-quick-guide/)

### Gaps

- Detailed ordering, publisher exclusions, repeated-author substitutions, and quotation punctuation edge cases were not independently checked against the complete MLA 9 handbook.
- Some accessible examples explicitly concern MLA 8; the skill discloses them in its source register rather than silently claiming current-edition proof.

## What changed for generative AI, and what should the bundle guarantee?

### Takeaway

The official AI citation revision is dated **13 August 2025**; acknowledgment and academic permission
remain distinct from citation mechanics. Check date: **2026-10-25**.

### Cited Findings

- The 2023 post explicitly sends readers to the revised guidance. — [Superseded article](https://style.mla.org/citing-generative-ai/)
- The revision adds specificity about model/version and prefers stable conversation links. It does not treat the AI tool as an author and advises consulting linked original sources. — [Revised guidance](https://style.mla.org/citing-generative-ai-updated-revised/)
- Substantive AI use can require acknowledgment beyond a bibliographic entry. — [Beyond Citation, 13 Aug. 2025](https://style.mla.org/describing-ai-use/)
- Students should follow instructor and institution AI policies. — [Student AI literacy](https://style.mla.org/student-guide-to-ai-literacy/)

### Inferences

- Citation instructions cannot confer course permission or justify inventing a human author; use verified metadata and local policy. — [Revised guidance](https://style.mla.org/citing-generative-ai-updated-revised/); [AI literacy](https://style.mla.org/student-guide-to-ai-literacy/)

### Gaps

- No institutional AI policy was supplied. A real student's permitted uses cannot be established from general MLA documentation.
- Bundle activation fixtures specify expected responses; they are not measured live-model activation results.
