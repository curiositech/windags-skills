# APA Formatting Research

## What edition and paper-layout rules should the skill teach?

### Takeaway

Checked 2026-10-25: APA seventh edition remains current. Student and professional
papers require different identification/header decisions, and online guidance
continues to evolve independently of the edition number.

### Cited Findings

- The seventh edition, released October 2019 and identified as the 2020 manual,
  remains current; the help page was updated July 2026. — [APA help](https://apastyle.apa.org/apa-style-help);
  [Manual](https://apastyle.apa.org/products/publication-manual-7th-edition)
- Student title pages identify course/instructor/due date; professional pages add
  author notes and research affiliations. — [Title page](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/title-page)
- Page numbering starts at 1 on the title page; professional running heads are
  uppercase and limited to 50 characters. — [Header](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/page-header)
- Student papers ordinarily omit abstracts unless requested. — [Order](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/order-pages)
- The font page, updated February 2026, includes 12-point Aptos among multiple
  permitted choices. — [Font](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/font)
- Defaults are one-inch margins, double-spacing with exceptions, and first-line
  indentation; reference paragraphs use hanging indents. — [Margins](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/margins);
  [Spacing](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/line-spacing);
  [Paragraphs](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/paragraph-format)
- Five hierarchical heading formats and sparse table rules require document
  styles, not plain-text simulation. — [Headings](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/headings);
  [Tables](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/tables-figures/tables)

### Inferences

- Implementation decision: separate student/professional branches early and
  report content checks separately from actual rendered-document inspection.
- A self-contained reference bundle is more useful than an unvalidated file
  generator for this initial scope.

### Gaps

- No DOCX/PDF was produced or visually inspected; the starter is a content scaffold.
- Detailed figures, dissertation-specific conventions, and publisher author-note
  requirements need targeted official follow-up for each real task.

## How should attribution and references handle edge cases?

### Takeaway

The skill must preserve evidence and distinguish in-text abbreviation from
reference-list author transcription. Missing source information requires explicit
adaptation, while unverified information remains a disclosed gap.

### Cited Findings

- Three or more authors abbreviate to first-author et al. in text from first
  mention; one/two authors and group-abbreviation cases differ. — [Author–date](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/basic-principles/author-date)
- Paraphrase locators are optional; direct quotes require a locator and quotations
  of 40 words or more use blocks. — [Paraphrases](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/paraphrasing);
  [Quotations](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/quotations)
- Headings/paragraphs or timestamps can replace unavailable pagination. — [Unpaginated quotation](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/quotations/no-page-numbers)
- Same-author/year suffixes follow reference ordering; consulted secondary works
  receive the reference entry, with “as cited in” attribution in text. — [Same author/date](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/basic-principles/same-year-author);
  [Secondary sources](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/secondary-sources)
- Personal exchanges are normally in-text only; the older APA Publishing article
  distinguishes recoverable material and research-participant data. — [Missing information](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/missing-information);
  [APA Publishing](https://blog.apapubs.org/2018/04/18/apa-style-central-expert-tip-citing-personal-communication/)
- References list up to 20 authors; 21+ use first 19, ellipsis, last author.
  Retrieval dates are restricted to changing unarchived content. — [Elements](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/elements-list-entry)
- DOI preference and database exceptions prevent copying arbitrary URLs into
  every reference. — [DOIs/URLs](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/dois-urls)
- Multiple-work parenthetical citations use alphabetical order and semicolons;
  same-author works use date order. — [Multiple works](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/citations/basic-principles/multiple-works)
- Edited-book chapters credit the chapter author; hosted user videos credit the
  uploading account. — [Chapters](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/edited-book-chapter-references);
  [Video](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/youtube-references)
- Core patterns vary by source type. — [Articles](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/journal-article-references);
  [Books](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/book-references);
  [Webpages](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/webpage-website-references)

### Inferences

- Implementation decision: require a source-metadata inventory and citation/reference
  reconciliation; never invent identifiers or locators to complete a pattern.
- Fictional labeled examples demonstrate formatting without passing invented works
  off as evidence.

### Gaps

- The dedicated current personal-communications page timed out repeatedly and was
  not read. The accessible current missing-information page and older official
  Publishing article provide the bounded support described above.
- An attempted dedicated unknown-author/date URL was inaccessible; exact shortened
  title citation typography is routed to official verification/manual Section 8.14.
- Full paid manual was not accessed. Complex legal, Indigenous-knowledge,
  translated/republished-work and dataset cases are outside the starter patterns.

## What AI guidance is current, and what does citation not authorize?

### Takeaway

Use the official September 2025 series rather than the archived 2023 recipe.
Permission to use AI comes from the relevant institution/publisher policy.

### Cited Findings

- The old ChatGPT post expressly marks itself archival and redirects readers. — [Archive notice](https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/how-to-cite-chatgpt)
- The current official reference post separates helpful specific chat references
  from general tool references. — [AI references](https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/cite-generative-ai-references)
- Instructor, institution, editor, and publisher policies govern allowed purposes
  and disclosure. — [AI allowed](https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/cite-generative-ai-allowed)

### Inferences

- Implementation decision: recheck current AI guidance at use time and never infer
  a model identity/date from a familiar product name.
- Citation should not expose confidential chat content; choose a suitable permitted
  disclosure approach for the actual assignment and audience.

### Gaps

- A third-party search result alleged April 2026 changes, but the official pages
  read show September 2025 creation dates. No unverified later revision is claimed.
- This research does not establish permission for any particular course/journal.
