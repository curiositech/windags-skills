# APA and MLA formatting skills

**Two separate skill bundles provide usable APA and MLA formatting workflows**: [APA Formatting](../skills/apa-formatting/SKILL.md) and [MLA Formatting](../skills/mla-formatting/SKILL.md), each with a short entry point, task-specific references, fictional worked examples, an output scaffold, and activation fixtures. The [APA notes](../research_notes/APA%20and%20MLA%20formatting%20skills/apa.md) and [MLA notes](../research_notes/APA%20and%20MLA%20formatting%20skills/mla.md) record official guidance checked on **25 October 2026**, supporting APA seventh edition and MLA ninth edition. Separating the bundles preserves the differences between APA author–date references and MLA author–locator citations and containers. Both require verified source metadata, assignment-specific overrides, and explicit reporting of unresolved information. This report documents the evidence and design choices supporting the skills; it is not itself an APA- or MLA-formatted paper. ([APA edition](https://apastyle.apa.org/products/publication-manual-7th-edition); [MLA edition](https://style.mla.org/mla-handbook-ninth-edition/))

## Separate entry points preserve each style’s decisions

The APA bundle starts by distinguishing student and professional papers, then routes layout, attribution, and reference construction to separate supporting files. This matters because title-page fields and running-head expectations differ by paper type; student papers do not inherit every professional-manuscript requirement. The MLA bundle instead centers its citation workflow on the work actually consulted, applicable core elements, and genuine containers. Explicit instructor or publisher requirements take precedence in the task, with departures from style defaults recorded rather than concealed. ([APA title pages](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/title-page); [APA headers](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/paper-format/page-header); [MLA core elements](https://style.mla.org/interactive-practice-template/))

**Metadata integrity precedes punctuation.** Both skills distinguish information genuinely absent from a source from information not supplied or verified. They prohibit invented authors, dates, identifiers, quotations, and locators; reconcile in-text keys with source lists; and keep unresolved placeholders out of artifacts described as submission-ready. Different missing-data conventions remain distinct: APA uses adaptations such as “n.d.” where appropriate, while MLA ordinarily omits unavailable date/page elements rather than inserting those placeholders. ([APA missing information](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/missing-information); [MLA missing information](https://style.mla.org/placeholders/))

## Official evidence supports bounded, refreshable guidance

The bundles include dated source registers so future users can revisit consequential rules. **Neither complete paid handbook was accessed.** APA’s dedicated current personal-communications page repeatedly timed out; the bundle discloses that gap and uses the accessible current missing-information table plus an older official APA Publishing explanation for its bounded treatment. MLA’s current formatting landing page was accessible, but its linked free ninth-edition chapter returned HTTP 403. The MLA layout baseline therefore identifies its archived official PDF and older posts as older evidence, with current ninth-edition sources independently supporting selected rules. These limits rule out a claim of exhaustive handbook verification. ([APA missing information](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/missing-information); [APA Publishing explanation](https://blog.apapubs.org/2018/04/18/apa-style-central-expert-tip-citing-personal-communication/); [MLA formatting route](https://style.mla.org/formatting-papers/); [archived MLA layout PDF](https://style.mla.org/app/uploads/sites/3/2020/12/Formatting-a-Research-Paper_v3_-The-MLA-Style-Center.pdf))

AI attribution uses the official updates observed in this research: APA’s September 2025 series and MLA’s **13 August 2025** revision, rather than frozen 2023 recipes. The skills require current verification when applying these changing rules and separate citation mechanics from institutional permission and disclosure requirements. A bibliographic entry cannot establish whether a course permits the underlying AI use. ([APA AI references](https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/cite-generative-ai-references); [APA AI permission](https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/cite-generative-ai-allowed); [MLA revised AI guidance](https://style.mla.org/citing-generative-ai-updated-revised/); [MLA acknowledgment](https://style.mla.org/describing-ai-use/))

## Review confirms boundaries without claiming runtime proof

Read-only review found the entry points, reference routing, fictional citation examples, and scaffold warnings consistent with the research notes. **Manual assessment** of each bundle’s five positive and five negative activation fixtures found its description and workflow support all twenty expected boundaries. Positive cases include paper formatting, citation repair, quotation decisions, and AI attribution; negative cases exclude other styles, ordinary email editing, unsupported research assumptions, and permission or certification claims the skill cannot establish. This assessment compares authored fixtures with instructions; it is not a live model invocation, measured activation test, or semantic-retrieval benchmark.

The templates intentionally supply content scaffolds rather than pretending Markdown can enforce document geometry. No final DOCX/PDF was produced or visually inspected during this work. Actual margins, headers, hanging indents, pagination, and MLA prose line counts require inspection of the destination document. Installation and catalog integration concern the two skill bundles; unchanged legacy MCP embeddings provide no evidence of semantic MCP coverage.

## Conclusion

The useful acceptance boundary for these skills is observable: a citation request yields a traceable entry and necessary caveats; a manuscript request additionally yields a clear account of source access and rendered checks. Future maintenance should prioritize newly revised official guidance and consequential edge cases, preserving the distinction between a supported formatting decision and a document whose final appearance has actually been inspected.
